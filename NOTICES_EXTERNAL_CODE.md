No third-party source code, models or datasets have been copied into this package. The included GitHub links are references to separately installed dependencies. Their licenses, copyrights and model/data terms remain with upstream owners. Before releasing as open-source or commercial software, the team must choose its own project license, review all upstream licenses and security vulnerabilities, and confirm rights for any dataset/model weights.
