Electronics (not needed for this video)

Nothing to wire

For this video you do not build the breadboard at all. The "sorting" is you placing the part in the right tray. You still film the whole story: measure, show, decide, route.

  • One camera and the digital caliper.
  • Three trays labeled PASS / HOLD / REJECT.
Keep it honest on camera: you move the part by hand; there is no automated gate in this version.

Later, if you get a servo

A servo diverter is an optional upgrade, not needed for the video. If you add one later, the full hole-by-hole wiring and the Power MB V2 setup are in physical_build_guide.html, and the firmware is firmware/manual_sorter/esp32_manual_sorter.ino.

Recording tips
  • Lock the camera on its stand and do not move it between parts, so every clip lines up.
  • One continuous take per part: place, measure aloud, show to camera, read the decision, place in the tray.
  • Say the three caliper numbers out loud; it makes the measurement legible on film.
  • Keep a visible caption like "manual walkthrough — part moved by hand". It keeps the demo honest and still shows the full decision flow.
  • Fill one row of the run log per part as you go; film the log too.
  • Record the good part first (clean PASS), then tight (HOLD), then anomaly and defect (REJECT). Judges follow the escalation easily.
