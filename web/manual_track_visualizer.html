    Works today with this repository

    • Printable specimens in examples/cad/stl/.
    • Servo firmware firmware/manual_sorter/esp32_manual_sorter.ino: stage P/H/R over serial, move only on a button press, 15 s expiry.
    • You stage the route by typing P/H/R in the Arduino Serial Monitor.
    • You record readings, captures, and the actual bin by hand in docs/engineering/templates/manual_track_run_log.csv.

    What this page is

    • A training and planning aid. Thresholds used for the illustrative decision are placeholders shown on screen.
    • The firmware emulator copies the sketch logic and message strings, so you can rehearse timing, held buttons, and expiry.
    • Nothing here is physical evidence. Hardware checks H1–H11 stay unchecked until you run them on your bench.
