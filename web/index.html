What we can and cannot claim

Supported, in simulation

  • The calibrated policy with risk-based pairing shipped fewer defective assemblies in 15/15 scenarios and kept throughput in 4/15.
  • When a camera reading goes missing or the network drops, it kept about 95 more good assemblies per hour than a rule that holds on missing data.
  • Optimising part matching against a pass/fail rule increased escapes under drift. It is safe only with a calibrated-risk objective.
  • Holding everything through one review station cut throughput by 82%.
  • Correcting the cost accounting reversed an earlier "peer requests save money" result. We published the correction.

Not claimed

  • Any factory measurement or proven saving. No physical run exists yet.
  • "21 agents collaborate". There are 21 roles, not 21 running models.
  • "8 NVIDIA systems running". Warp is used; the other adapters are NOT_CONFIGURED.
  • A payback period or annual saving.
